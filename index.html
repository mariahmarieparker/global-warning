The Reality of Global Warming

Global temperatures are rising, causing severe weather, melting arctic sea ice, and destroying ecosystems. Living things are intricately connected; when a climate shift alters water temperatures or destroys forests, entire marine webs and terrestrial habitats collapse simultaneously. Real change requires systematic policy shifts alongside immediate funding to protect frontline habitats and displaced populations.

About The Creator

Hi, my name is Mariah Parker.

I am a 15 year old environmental advocate and a beginner independent web developer. I designed Global Warning because most climate websites only focus on massive global organizations. Global Warning is designed to let users split their impact between vetted NGOs and direct direct-aid funds for families and endangered animals displaced by climate disasters.

Scientific accuracy and living beings' wellbeing is my highest priority. I hope to provide clear, actionable tracking tools that empower individuals to make a tangible impact on our planet's future.
