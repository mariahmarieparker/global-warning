The Reality of Global Warming

Global temperatures are rising, causing severe weather, melting arctic sea ice, and destroying ecosystems. Living things are intricately connected; when a climate shift alters water temperatures or destroys forests, entire marine webs and terrestrial habitats collapse simultaneously. Real change requires systematic policy shifts alongside immediate funding to protect frontline habitats and displaced populations.
